## What Is Metaphor: ReFantazio?

Metaphor: ReFantazio is a fantasy role-playing game developed by Studio Zero and published by ATLUS. It combines turn-based battles, real-time combat actions, character customization, dungeon journeys, and a story shaped by political rivalry.

The adventure takes place in the United Kingdom of Euchronia, where a royal succession contest sets the central events in motion. Players travel across varied locations, meet allies, face hostile creatures, and take part in challenges that affect their journey through the kingdom.

A defining feature is the Archetype system. It lets party members adopt different combat roles, giving players options for building a team and adapting to the demands of each encounter.
